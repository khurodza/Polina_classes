Лицей НИУ ВШЭ · English · вступительные испытания в 10 класс · + ОГЭ

28 lessons of 90 minutes, two a week from the week of 30 November 2026, plus 2 reserve lessons. The course ends just before the written exam (essay) in the second half of March 2027. The main aim is the opinion essay of the second stage. Every lesson is built on one Gateway to the World B2 unit (topic vocabulary and grammar), and that unit’s topic is the topic of the lesson’s essay work from «Хочу в Лицей! Пишу эссе на 10 баллов» (Иванова, Шиловская, 2020; the PDF in ~/Documents/CLASSES/Materials/). A 15-minute test strand in every lesson trains the English part of the first-stage комплексный тест (headings, word formation, grammar), which is also the grammar and word-formation core of the ОГЭ. For the ОГЭ, every Gateway lesson also has 10 minutes of listening (the unit’s own tracks and videos) and 10 minutes of speaking (an ОГЭ monologue on the lesson’s topic).

Dates: the 2027 admission rules usually come out in December–January. In the 2026 campaign the комплексный тест (remote) was on 5–6 March and the essay (in person, 40 minutes) on 22–27 March. The plan assumes the same pattern: mock tests in the lessons before early March, the final essay lessons in mid-March. Section 7 explains how to shorten the plan if the dates move.

Lesson codes. Each lesson is numbered with the Gateway B2 Student’s Book unit it is based on: Unit 1 → Lessons 1.1, 1.2, 1.3; Unit 2 → 2.1, 2.2; … Unit 10 → 10.1, 10.2. The exam lessons after the units are E.1–E.6, the reserves R.1–R.2. Each topic (Gateway unit) has one flashcard deck with the unit’s number, as the Olympiad decks V8–V13 had: deck 1 · School & education serves Lessons 1.1–1.3, and so on; the Essay language deck holds the phrases of cards T1–T6. Stage 2 · The essay (10 points)

You should spend about 40 minutes on the task. Write an opinion essay on the following topic:

Video games are important for developing problem-solving skills. However, many people think differently.

Discuss both these views and give your own opinion. Write your essay in 150–200 words (±10%).

Use the following plan: 1. Introduction: state the problem; identify the two opinions given in the task. 2. Main Body: Body paragraph 1: point of view 1 + 1 or 2 reasons to support it; Body paragraph 2: point of view 2 + 1 or 2 reasons to support it. 3. Conclusion: summarise the main points of your essay and state your opinion.

  • Word count. 150–200 words, ±10%. Under 135 words the essay is not marked (0). Over 200, the examiner counts the first 200 words and marks only those, so a long essay loses its conclusion and with it the opinion (К1).
  • Time. 40 minutes, in person, by hand. From Lesson 4.1 every timed essay is handwritten.
  • К1 = 0 makes the whole essay 0. This happens if: there is no introduction and conclusion, or no main body; the essay is about a problem the task does not set; it is another format (a letter, an article, an essay of another type); there are fewer than 135 words; more than 30% of it matches a published text or another applicant’s essay.
  • The 30% rule: learned phrases (frames, linkers) are fine. Whole learned paragraphs are not. The book itself says the same about its model essays (Ch. 12).

What this means for the course

  • The book teaches an older, one-sided plan; the 2026 task is two-sided. In the book (Ch. 2) the introduction gives the writer’s opinion and both body paragraphs support it. Ch. 7 even lists “writing for and against” as a wrong format. The 2026 plan is the opposite: the introduction presents both views, body paragraph 1 = view 1, body paragraph 2 = view 2, and the opinion is given in the conclusion. We use the book’s method (Ch. 3–7) and its exercises, but always in the 2026 plan. The new trap is the old format: a one-sided essay gets К1 = 1.
  • Both views must be argued fully. “Full arguments in support of both views, with relevant examples” is the К1 wording, so she must be able to argue the side she disagrees with. Every brainstorm in this course has two columns.
  • The opinion lives in the conclusion, and it must be clear. Book Ch. 4 (opinion statement) now trains the last paragraph. “Everyone should decide for themselves” counts as no opinion (К1 = 1). A nuanced opinion is fine if it is clear: “I believe that video games are useful as long as…”.
  • Paraphrase is worth a point. Copying the prompt into the introduction costs a К1 point. From Lesson 1.2 she keeps a synonym bank (Ch. 4 Зад. 4).
  • Accuracy decides К3–К5, which give 5 of the 10 points. One lexical error is allowed for full К3, two grammar errors for full К4, two spelling or punctuation errors for К5. Hence the Error log, the book’s Ch. 8–10, and 7 minutes of checking in every timed essay.
  • Range also counts. К3 = 1 and К4 = 1 also go to error-free essays written with only A2 words or only simple sentences. This is why every lesson’s Gateway grammar is turned into essay sentences (passive, conditionals, relative clauses, gerund subjects, comparatives, reporting verbs).
  • Topics come from the official list (предметное содержание): family and conflicts, friends, appearance and character, free time (sport, music, reading, theatre, museums, travel), shopping and pocket money, school life and uniform, choosing a profession, foreign languages, countries and outstanding people, travel and transport, technology and gadgets (parental control), mass media, nature, ecology, city and country, healthy lifestyle. Every one of them is covered by a Gateway unit below.

3. How a lesson runs

  • Standard lesson (90 min): Review & Error log 7 · Gateway input 18 · Listening 10 · Speaking (ОГЭ monologue) 10 · Test strand 15 · Essay workshop 30.
  • Timed-essay lesson (Lessons 4.1, 6.2, 8.2, 10.1): Gateway input 10 · Listening 8 · Speaking 7 · Test strand 15 · Timed essay 40 · Self-check with the К1–К5 sheet 10. The exam lessons (Lessons 23–28) have their own shape, each with a short monologue.
  • Listening (10 min) in every Gateway lesson: the unit’s own listening track or its Great Learners, Great Thinkers video, on the lesson’s topic. Before: predict; while: the Gateway task + notes of every opinion and reason heard, which feed the essay’s two columns. Four Exam success listenings (SB pp. 144–148: multiple choice, note-taking, matching) are set at home in the ОГЭ manner: heard twice, answers written.
  • Speaking (10 min) in every Gateway lesson: one ОГЭ monologue (Задание 3 of the oral part) on the lesson’s topic: three points, 1.5 minutes to plan, up to 2 minutes and 10–12 sentences (card T9). She plans on a small card, speaks against the timer, then gets one thing to fix: coverage of all three points, length, linkers, the lesson’s new words. The unit’s Developing speaking page (SB pp. 14, 26, 40, 52, 66, 78, 92, 104, 118, 130; model tracks 4, 9, 14, 21, 26, 32, 40, 46, 52, 59) supplies the phrases. The monologue uses the same topic as the essay, so it doubles as a spoken brainstorm.
  • Flashcards: every topic is a deck on the Flashcards page (deck 1 · School & education … deck 10 · News, media & languages, 25–27 cards each, from the unit’s Vocabulary in context boxes + the topic words the essays need), as the Olympiad decks V8–V13 were. The deck is taught in the unit’s first lesson (fc-app → fc-list → “Choose the word that fits”), practised in the unit’s other lessons and at home. The Essay language deck (30 cards) is taught card by card with T1 (1.2), T5 (2.2), T3 (3.1), T6 (5.2) and T4 (7.2). Old Olympiad decks are in a folder on the same page.
  • Gateway input is either the unit’s vocabulary (the topic deck) or its grammar. The grammar is always taken one step further, into essay sentences on the lesson’s topic: if she learns the passive, she writes the passive sentences of today’s introduction.
  • Test strand (15 min) is one task of the комплексный тест on the lesson’s topic, rotating headings → word formation → grammar MC. Mini-mock of all three tasks in Lesson 5.2, full mocks in Lessons 9.2 and E.1. Every third task is also set at home in the open ОГЭ form (no options: she writes the form herself).
  • Essay workshop (35 min) is one book chapter or section, done on the lesson’s topic, ending with a piece she writes in class (an intro, a body paragraph, a conclusion, or a plan).
  • Homework (60–75 min): a 2-minute recorded monologue on the lesson’s topic once a week (sent as a voice message) · flashcards 10 · book exercises 15–20 · essay 35–45 (from Lesson 2.2 a full essay, timed at home, photographed and uploaded; from Lesson 4.2 handwritten) · corrections of the last essay 10.

Marking and tracking

  • Every essay gets К1–К5 scores (out of 10) and error codes in the margin: L lexical · G grammar · S spelling · P punctuation · St style/register · Lk linker · Log logic · WO word order · ✓✓ a phrase worth keeping. She corrects the coded errors herself before the next lesson.
  • Error log: a running list grouped by the book’s categories (Ch. 8 lexical, Ch. 9 grammar, Ch. 10 spelling). Each lesson opens with 3–5 of her own errors to fix from memory. From Lesson E.1 it becomes her personal Top-10 for the check stage.
  • Score table: date · topic · words · К1 · К2 · К3 · К4 · К5 · total. Timed essay 1 (4.1) and the demo essay mock (E.5) are the two ends of the line.
