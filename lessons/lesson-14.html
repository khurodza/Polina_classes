👩‍🏫 Teacher only · what to listen for
  • Past continuous for the main events: *Then I was going to the station → Then I went. It is the background only: I was waiting when…
  • No past perfect for “before”: *When I arrived, the train already left → had already left.
  • State verbs in the continuous: *I was knowing / was having a dog → knew / had.
  • Ago and before: *before two years → two years ago; two years earlier / before inside the story.
  • Used to / would for old habits: I used to be scared of… / Every summer we would…
  • Ask one follow-up question that needs the past perfect: “What had you expected before you got there?”
